About

Hey, I'm Hillary — a visual artist based in Nairobi, Kenya.

My practice spans installation, printmaking, photography, and time-based media. I'm drawn to work that sits at the edge of what language can hold — images and objects that carry feeling more faithfully than words.

My work is shaped by questions around the body, memory, and belonging. What survives a moment. What is passed down without being named. I work with materials that hold their own histories — cloth, earth, glass, light.

I started making things seriously in my early twenties, moving between self-taught processes and formal study. After completing an MFA in Visual Arts, I returned to Nairobi to build a studio practice rooted in East African contexts while remaining in conversation with a wider world.

My work has been shown across Kenya and internationally, in group shows, residencies, and self-organised exhibitions. I am always open to collaboration, commissions, and new conversations.
